What is in the report

  • Talking points — a timed 45-minute run-sheet: uptake evidence, the product families with live fee benchmarks, who is winning by country, and a potential shelf.

  • Every firm — 613 rows, filterable by region, country, type and product.
  • Counterparty map — which bullion banks and custodians each wealth manager works with, and where none is disclosed.

How to read the confidence tags

Nothing here should go on a slide without checking its tag. Product cells marked Y were verified on the firm's own page or a primary document; Y? is known but unverified. Every size figure carries a source link and a quote, while size bands are estimates and are labelled as such. In the run-sheet, each line carries the verdict of an independent fact-check: 37 confirmed, 34 partly verified, 16 corrected in the text, and 3 that could not be independently supported.

Figures are as of the dates stated in each record, largely 2025 annual reports and 2026 interim data. The prescriptive sections are illustrative strategic ideas, not anyone's plan or recommendation.
