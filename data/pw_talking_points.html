Summary↑

Every large wealth franchise sells gold to its clients in some form. One global franchise's client-facing metals shelf is one paper-gold product in one booking centre.

  • 613 private banks and wealth managers worldwide, 540 of them private-bank or wealth arms. 564 named client products across 232 firms.
  • Citi is an LBMA market maker, became the fifth member of London clearing (LPMCL) on 6 July 2026, appears on the LBMA list of London vaulting custodians, and Citibank N.A. carries the second-largest US precious-metals derivative book: US$236bn notional at 31 March 2026 vs JPMorgan US$509bn (year-end 2025: Citi US$217bn, JPMorgan US$479bn, Bank of America US$121bn). src

Gold account, unallocated
94 in catalogue
A claim on the bank for a number of grams or ounces, with no specific bars set aside. Priced off the LBMA benchmark or the bank's own two-way price, and hedged in the interbank market. The cheapest kind to run, and usually carries no right to take delivery.Swiss metal-account fees run from 0.20% a year at Luzerner Kantonalbank, through 0.30% at Basler Kantonalbank for gold, to 0.80% on the former Credit Suisse book now inside UBS. UOB Gold Savings charges 0.25% a year. Emirates NBD gram gold charges a 2.1% arrangement fee below USD 250,000, plus a one-off 0.315% custody and insurance charge if the client redeems within five years, or 1.05% after that. HSBC Wayfoong Statement Gold caps the spread at 4%. Citi Hong Kong Gold Manager charges the spread only.
Metal account, allocated
26 in catalogue
The client owns identified bars, or a pro-rata share of a pool, held in a named vault. The bank earns a custody fee and the dealing spread.UBS key4 gold charges 0.35% a year for storage, from 0.1 g. Intesa Deposito Oro charges 0.75% a year. Royal Canadian Mint exchange-traded receipts charge 0.35% for gold and 0.45% for silver, and BMO's Gold Deposit Program custodies at the same Mint. JPMorgan Private Bank holds client gold in its own vault, with unallocated claims from around USD 250,000 and allocated 400 oz bars from around USD 1m, plus storage and insurance.
Physical bars and coins
135 in catalogue
Sold over the counter, in the app, or delivered, then either stored by the bank or taken away. The volume driver in Asia and Germany. The buy-back policy is effectively the product: OCBC Hong Kong withdrew bar buy-back on 1 February 2026.Sold by Reisebank, ZKB, UOB, Shinhan, Emirates NBD under its own brand, and the Canadian bank online stores. Fidelity, through FideliTrade, charges 2.90% down to 0.99% to buy and 2.00% down to 0.75% to sell, with storage at 0.125% a quarter.
Savings and accumulation plans
32 in catalogue
Monthly accumulation in grams or whole coins from a small ticket. The entry product for mass-affluent clients, and the feeder into the account business.Reisebank's Goldsparplan starts at EUR 25 a month and Austrian Sparkassen coin plans at EUR 50. Garanti funds its plan from the client's credit-card limit. Bank Syariah Indonesia takes subscriptions from 0.02 g. Volumes on Bank of Taiwan's regular plan grew 40% in 2025.
Digital gold and tokens
13 in catalogue
A tokenised claim on vaulted gold, dealt around the clock in fractional units. The retail growth product of 2025 and 2026 in Hong Kong and Singapore.HSBC Gold Token trades in 0.001 oz units with no dealing or storage fee, but a bank margin of up to 2%, or 5% outside local trading hours, and no right to delivery; more than USD 1bn had traded by November 2025, with the bullion vaulted in London. DBS announced a 1 g token held in its own Singapore vault in June 2026. AMINA lends against its gold token.
Gold deposits and dual-currency investments
16 in catalogue
A term product with two common shapes. Either the client's gold earns a yield because the bank lends it on, or a cash deposit converts into gold at maturity if the price passes an agreed level. Uncommon outside Turkey, China and Malaysia, and the kind of product a bullion desk prices directly.China Merchants Bank's 金生利 pays the yield in grams of gold. Kuveyt Türk runs a gold-to-gold participation account. CIMB's Gold Convertible treats gold as the second currency. HSBC China sells gold-linked structured deposits, and Citi Hong Kong offers Gold Premium Investment.
Structured notes on gold
66 in catalogue
Autocallables, reverse convertibles and capital-protected notes referencing the gold price. Distributed through the private-bank advisory channel and hedged by the issuing desk.Vontobel carried CHF 1.33bn of physical metal at the end of 2025 to hedge its metal-linked products. Citi issues market-linked notes on the SPDR Gold Trust for the Wells Fargo Advisors shelf.
Lending against metal
20 in catalogue
Borrowing secured on bullion, on a metal account, or on metal exchange-traded funds. Rare among UK and US banks.Swiss houses lend against metal accounts and bullion but do not publish advance rates by asset type. Money Metals lends up to 65% against bullion in the US. AMINA extends credit lines against its own gold token. ANZ Investment Lending accepts the ASX-listed gold products as collateral.
Own funds, ETFs and the house allocation
54 in catalogue
The house implements its published view on gold through a vehicle it controls, which is how discretionary mandates get the exposure.ANZ Private seeded a State Street gold fund charging 0.14% in July 2024 and maintains an overweight. JPMorgan Private Bank recommends around 5%. HSBC Global Private Banking is overweight gold. Coutts and ABN AMRO have both added gold sleeves to their model portfolios.

A potential shelf↑

Three tiers. Each maps to a competitor product that already exists.

  • Tier 1 (private bank, all booking centres): an allocated precious-metals account in the group's own London vault with physical delivery via a secure-logistics partner, plus Lombard lending against it. Template: JPMorgan PB vault claims; Julius Baer / LGT metal accounts.
  • Tier 2 (affluent and private-client tiers in SG, HK, UAE, UK): an unallocated gram-gold account with physical redemption in kilobars or 100 g, priced off loco-London and desk-hedged. Template: UOB Gold Savings, Emirates NBD gram gold, HSBC Wayfoong Statement Gold; an existing paper-gold product could be extended beyond one booking centre and given a delivery right.
  • Tier 3 (yield and structured): gold-linked deposits and a gold DCI where a lease book funds the client yield; a standing gold autocall/reverse-convertible programme; a house strategic-allocation view implemented via a physically backed vehicle. Templates: CMB, Kuveyt Türk, CIMB Gold Convertible, HSBC China structured deposits, ANZ Private gold fund.
  • KPIs to set now: metal-account balances and account count by booking centre, physical delivered (kg), Lombard drawn against metal, structured-note issuance on XAU, share of PB clients holding any metal product.
